## User scenarios and acceptance

1. P1 — From Home or Team, enter Projects and browse six keyboard-accessible illustrated cards. Show three columns on desktop, two on tablet and one on mobile. Each card identifies the event and year. Four carry the supplied awards.
2. P1 — Open a card or a direct project URL, refresh it, and navigate back to the originating card with its focus and scroll position. The document title and active navigation identify the current page. An unknown slug offers recovery.
3. P2 — Explore Risk Miner's capabilities, three concept views, stack and demonstration team. All other projects share a compact detail view. Omit absent optional sections.

## Requirements and success criteria

Match the Projects reference hierarchy, layout, artwork subjects and headline at five requested viewport sizes. Verify no horizontal overflow, runtime errors or lost keyboard targets. Record observed FPS and the measurement environment.

## 04B visual refinement

Visitors should see the same portfolio content against a legible, individually framed orbital scene. At 1920×1080 all twelve team cards should fit in two rows with readable roles and technology icons. Project artwork should sit to the right of the copy, with aligned footers and clear awards. Standard details need a near-square art treatment. In Risk Miner the map takes precedence over the station, its edges blend into the hero, and HackNation is visibly an event. Keep existing data and assets.

Clarified from the supplied brief: use event names where licensed organiser marks are absent. Smaller screens may scroll. Use live frame samples for performance, direct screenshots to diagnose layout and the recordings for composition.
