# Demonstration assets

All twelve portraits depict fictional adults generated with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-11-25. Prompts requested chest-up professional portraits, natural individual faces, dark modern clothing, cool blue key lighting, red rim lighting and a dark navy background, without text or logos. Individual prompts varied age, hair, facial hair and glasses. Local files are optimized WebP copies.

Technology logos are original Devicon SVGs from https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/master/icons, retrieved 2026-11-25. The included icons/LICENSE preserves the upstream MIT license. Brand colors are retained. Generic concept icons are project-authored SVGs. Labels identify each technology for assistive technology.
